Store Policies

Everything you need to know about shopping with Wumii Luxe, payment protection, refunds, and dispatch delivery.

01 / BUYER PROTECTION

Refund & Exchange Policy

  • 100% Out-of-Stock Refund Guarantee: If you complete a bank transfer for any item that becomes unavailable or sold out in our store before dispatch, 100% of your money will be refunded promptly to your bank account—or, if you prefer, you may swap it for another available item of equal value.
  • Inspection Upon Delivery: Please inspect your wears, bags, shoes, slides, heels, or Islamic outfits immediately upon arrival of our dispatch rider.
  • Exchange Window: Items are eligible for size or defective-item exchange within 24 to 48 hours of delivery, provided the item remains unworn, unwashed, with all original tags and packaging intact.
  • Hygiene & Final Sale Items: For hygiene reasons, scarves, scarf pins/accessories, and clearance items cannot be returned or exchanged unless damaged prior to delivery.
  • How to Request a Refund or Exchange: Message us on WhatsApp at +234 803 990 2723 with your transfer receipt and a clear photo or video of the item within 24 hours of delivery.
02 / DISPATCH & LOGISTICS

Shipping & Delivery Policy

  • Order Processing: Orders are confirmed and scheduled for dispatch immediately after your bank transfer payment is verified via WhatsApp.
  • Delivery Coverage: We deliver to any location using trusted local dispatch riders and reliable nationwide courier services.
  • Delivery Fees: Dispatch rider fees depend on your specific delivery address and will be confirmed with you on WhatsApp right after you submit your order.
03 / DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

  • Information We Collect: When you place an order on Wumii Luxe, we collect your full name, phone/WhatsApp number, and delivery address solely to process and deliver your purchase.
  • How Your Information Is Used: Your contact and location details are shared strictly with our verified dispatch riders to complete your delivery. We never sell, rent, or share your personal details with third-party advertisers.
  • Payment Security: All payments are completed directly via bank transfer. Wumii Luxe never asks for or stores debit card numbers, PINs, or online banking passwords on this website.
